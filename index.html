Learn Unsupervised with Phoebe

Finding structure with no answer key

Mango Lane has 12,000 customers and no labels. Ten builder sessions turn that pile into named, fundable segments - real k-means, DBSCAN, PCA, and basket mining running live in your browser - while teaching the skepticism that keeps you from naming noise. The unlabeled half of the machine learning zoo, with the traps left armed so you can spring them safely.

k-means from the inside

Step Lloyd's loop by hand: assign, update, repeat until still. Inertia, k-means++ starts, the unlucky initialization that locks into a worse answer - and the n_init default old tutorials still get wrong.

The workhorse 🎛 Session 4 · building

Picking k: the question with no answer key

Silhouette read fluently, the elbow method named and demoted, the actionability test - and the "More segments!" trap button that turns a 0.68 into a 0.34 while looking more sophisticated.

✨ The k-trapInteractive 🌙 Session 5 · building

Beyond k-means

Two interleaved moons k-means cannot see (83%) and DBSCAN traces perfectly (100%), dendrograms, GMM's soft maybe-both answers - and how to choose between them like the sklearn comparison table does.

The rivals 🗜 Session 6 · building

Compressing the customer

Real PCA: three features become two axes carrying 86% of the variance. Plus the honesty split the field keeps fumbling - PCA compresses, t-SNE and UMAP visualize, and embedding distances lie.

Dimensionality 🏷 Session 7 · building

Naming and shipping segments

From cluster ids to personas with owners: profiling tables, stability checks, the segment sheet artifact - and why naming before validating is the most enjoyable mistake in data science.

The deliverable 🧺 Session 8 · building

What sells together

Association rules mined live from 500 Mango Lane baskets: support, confidence, and lift - why lift is the honest column, and the sneakers-to-socks 3.0x rule your cross-sell email writes itself from.

Basket mining 🎭 Session 9 · deepening

When clustering lies

Run 5-means on pure noise and watch it answer confidently - then learn the tells: the silhouette gap, sklearn's own assumptions demo, drift, and the professional judgment of when NOT to cluster.

Ship it
easiest - foundations building - the craft deepening - the skeptic capstone - ship it
🥭 The running case: Mango Lane's 12,000 customers. One brief from marketing - "give us segments we can campaign to" - carries all ten sessions. The lab's 300-customer sample has five planted archetypes; you recover them badly (raw cents), then perfectly (scaled), validate that five is real, name them, mine their baskets, and ship the segment sheet. The same twelve customers you hand-sort in session 1 come back graded in the capstone.
The honesty rail. The lab data is simulated - that is the only reason a "truth match" score can exist, and every page says so. Everything else is real: real k-means++ (best of five restarts), real DBSCAN, real spherical-GMM EM, real Jacobi PCA, real silhouette, real rule mining - all computed in your browser on every click. And where a topic has its own course on this shelf (anomaly detection, recommenders, feature engineering), we point instead of re-teaching.
